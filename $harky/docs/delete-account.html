Delete your account and data

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Delete your data from inside the app

Open $harky, go to Settings → Data → Delete all data. This permanently removes, immediately:

  • All financial data stored on your device (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals and contributions).
  • If you are signed in with Google and have sync enabled, the same data stored in our cloud database (Supabase) linked to your account.

This does not require contacting us and takes effect right away.

2. Request full account deletion

If you also want your Google sign-in link and account record removed entirely (not just your financial data), send an email to contactosharklin@gmail.com from the email address associated with your $harky account, with the subject "Delete account".

We will process the request within 30 days and confirm by email once it is done.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your account identifier, name, email and profile photo received from Google sign-in.
  • All financial data (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, contributions) stored in our cloud database.
  • Any comments or feedback you sent us through the app, where reasonably possible.

4. What may be retained

We may keep minimal records (such as the fact that a deletion request was made and its date) for a limited period, only as needed to comply with legal obligations or to prevent abuse. No financial data is retained for this purpose.
