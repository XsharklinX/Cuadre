Delete your data

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Cuadre has no accounts

There is no sign-in, no user record and no server. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own device, so there is no account for us to delete and no copy of your data for us to hold.

1. Delete your data from inside the app

Open Cuadre, go to Settings → Data → Delete all data. This permanently removes, immediately, every account, transaction, category, budget, goal and contribution stored on your device.

This does not require contacting us and takes effect right away.

2. Or simply uninstall

Uninstalling Cuadre removes the app's private storage along with it, which deletes everything the app ever held. Backup files you exported yourself are the one exception: those live wherever you saved them, and only you can delete them.

3. Comments you emailed us

If you sent us a comment or a support request by email, write to contactosharklin@gmail.com and we will delete that correspondence where reasonably possible. It is the only thing about you we could ever have.
